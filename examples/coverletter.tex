\lettersection{About Me}
I am a Machine Learning Engineer with a Master’s degree in Information Technology from the University of Melbourne and a deep interest in computer vision for real-world applications. I’ve worked across drone vision projects, road safety detection, and environmental object tracking. I’m someone who values clear goals, careful planning, and follows through on commitments—I like to keep communication straightforward and results-focused.

\lettersection{Why Cyberhawk?}
Cyberhawk’s work speaks directly to both my interests and strengths. The mission of improving industrial safety through drone-based computer vision solutions is not only exciting but also aligns with the kind of problems I enjoy solving. I see a strong overlap between your use of drone-captured imagery for infrastructure inspection and the projects I’ve delivered using aerial and ground-level visual data. I also appreciate your open, no-drama culture and plainspoken communication—it’s the kind of environment where I believe I’d do my best work.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
Most recently at KJR, I contributed to Feral AI, a system that uses drone imagery to detect objects in the environment. Prior to that, I worked as an ML engineering intern where I developed models to identify potholes and cracks from road images. I am experienced with PyTorch, OpenCV, and custom pipelines for image dataset preparation and defect detection. I’ve deployed models in real-world systems, handled noisy drone footage, and iteratively improved detection performance through data analysis and model tuning. I’m confident my skills in computer vision, paired with a practical, hands-on mindset, would make me a strong contributor to your team from day one.

\end{cvletter}
