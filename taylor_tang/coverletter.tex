Your distributed-first approach to work resonates deeply with me—having collaborated successfully across remote teams throughout my career. What particularly excites me is the opportunity to build and maintain backend services that serve machine learning models at Atlassian's scale, directly supporting teams worldwide in making data-driven decisions. The role's focus on operationalizing ML models, designing robust data pipelines, and ensuring system reliability speaks to my core strengths in backend engineering and MLOps. I'm drawn to contributing to architecture discussions and proactively identifying technical improvements that will help Atlassian teams collaborate more effectively.
  
  \lettersection{Why Me?}
  I am uniquely qualified for this position through my strong software engineering foundation combined with hands-on experience deploying machine learning models into production environments. My technical expertise directly aligns with your requirements: extensive experience building and maintaining backend services and APIs, solid understanding of data workflows including pipeline design and transformations, and proficiency in writing and optimizing SQL queries for analytics and reporting. 
  
  Throughout my career, I've collaborated closely with data scientists and ML engineers to operationalize models, ensuring they meet performance, reliability, and scalability requirements in production. My experience delivering end-to-end ML pipelines—from data processing and orchestration to model deployment—using cloud platforms like AWS and GCP demonstrates my ability to build robust, maintainable systems. What sets me apart is my naturally curious mindset; I enjoy exploring systems architecture, identifying inefficiencies, and implementing thoughtful automation to improve reliability and observability. My growth-oriented approach drives me to continuously learn new tools and technologies while maintaining focus on solid engineering principles.
